Limbus Company

Project Moon's third game,the player is Dante,a amnesiatic who is recruited as a "sinner" by Limbus company, themselves leading the other 12 sinners who corrolate to characters from various pieces of literature, with the stated goal of collecting the golden boughs given to them.

Each main story chapter is a "Canto" which covers one of the 12 sinners, each being a loose adapation of their source material within the confines of the game's setting as well as the layers of hell as depicted in Dante's inferno. Shorter side stories known as "Intervallos" are present between main chapters, typically focusing either on smaller elements of the world or being a prelude to a upcoming character's story.

The game features a unique blend of dark fantasy and psychological horror, exploring themes of guilt, redemption, and the nature of sin is explored among both the main characters and their supporting cast, some familiar faces from the books, some unique.

The player is also in direct opposition to two main groups in N corp's new league of nine, a group with a similar aim to Limbus lead by Hermann, and the enigmatic mysterious blue group lead by Demian.

Library of Ruina

Project Moon's second game, the story is set after the events of Lobotomoy corporation where previous game's deuroprotagonist Angelaworks alongside a claimed low ranking Fixer named Roland to elevate the library to the top of the city and make Angela human.

Along the way, they will fight various people or groups of note in the city, meet new Librarians, characters from the previous game, and uncover a dark plot relating to a man who Roland knows all too well.

The game itself is a visual novel and deck building card game, where after each successful battle or defeat the player will gain a page, which can be burnt or used to progress to later stages, when burnt the player will be granted a new set of cards or even a keypage which is used to make the player's character look like one of those they defeated and grants unique buffs,passives, and weakness

Underneath the crueling and bleak world is a story of hope and reconsolidation, with the games difficulty adding to the themes of the game and it's world building.

Touhou

The Touhou Project is a series of bullet hell shoot 'em up games created by a single developer known as "Team Shanghai Alice". The games are known for their challenging gameplay and rich lore.
